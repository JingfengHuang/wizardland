Events: Experience the Flow of Magic

Come experience our magical events. Your life will never be the same.

Life has held magical power from the beginning, but people have not yet discovered it. Our wizards will help you learn and use different spells.

"Do these things really exist, or did they happen only in my mind?"

Invisibility

What if you were invisible? Would you play tricks on others or do something unbelievable? In Wizard Land, you can become just like smoke, scattered by the wind.

This event teaches you how to become invisible and how to return to normal. There is no fixed schedule, and you can learn at any time. By the way, our wizards can still find you.

"Do not worry; you will not really be burned to ashes."

Illusion Maze

Have you ever tried to challenge a maze? In the magical world, a maze is no longer ordinary or fixed. The illusion maze is built with spells, and success requires teamwork, patience, and shared wisdom.

The maze levels are illusions generated by spells, and they change constantly as the game progresses. Players must adjust their strategy in real time, or they will fail.

"If you never find the exit, does that mean you will be trapped inside forever?"

Create Your Own Planet

Use spells to create your own planet, including animals, plants, and anything else you can imagine.

You can create anything you like, including animals, plants, rocks, and metals that do not exist on Earth. You can even create movement that ignores the laws of physics or chemical reactions that defy common sense.

"What if Earth was created this way?"

Lucky Spell

Everyone wants to be luckier, but what if there were a spell that allowed you to avoid all bad luck? That is the luck spell.

This spell lasts all day, which means nothing will happen that goes against your expectations. In other words, for one day, you will feel almost omnipotent.

"If your opponent is a wizard, your luck may run out."

More Events Are Waiting for You

Everyone is welcome to visit at any time. More events are waiting for you!

In addition to daily activities, you can enter your expected departure date under 'Future Events' in the Account menu to view more events for that month. These are not daily activities, so we recommend planning your itinerary carefully.
