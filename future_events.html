Receive a Magic Cat

Every traveler can receive a magic cat for free. It can understand its owner's words, do basic chores, and protect its owner in critical moments.

Sacrificial Ceremony

In Wizard Land, a sacrificial ceremony is held every year on January 10. A bonfire lights up the night sky in memory of the great magician Mar, who used his lifelong knowledge to create a barrier that protects Wizard Land.

Magic Chess

Magic Chess is one of Wizard Land's traditional events. In this event, the chess pieces are no longer ordinary pieces; they become living kings, queens, rooks, knights, and more. They strictly follow the player's orders, and every traveler can become a chess player and pursue the title of "Light of Wisdom."
