FAQ

Q: Will there be any harmful spells in Wizard Land?

A: No. There are no harmful spells in Wizard Land, and our wizards will do their best to protect you.

Q: What is the exact address of Wizard Land? How can I get there?

A: As mentioned on the 'Explore-Castle' page, Wizard Land is hidden, so it has no public address. The only way to get there is by teleportation. Open the 'Explore-Teleport' page on your departure date, and you will be drawn into the portal with your friends or family and arrive at our resort immediately.

Q: How long will the trip be?

A: It depends on your preference. You may stay as long as you like, though most travelers choose to stay for one month.

Q: Do I have a chance to become a real wizard in Wizard Land and join the guardians of this land?

A: Yes. If you are willing to leave your previous life behind and are determined to learn and grow here, we welcome you to become one of us.
