Wizard Castle: A Hidden Place

Wizard Castle is a hidden place where wizards live and unexpected things happen every day.

Taste food that changes with your mood, sleep in the air, or even become someone else without being noticed!

Even our wizards do not know all the secrets here.

"Only people who can see this website can see our hidden place."

Automatically Cooked Food

Live with everything prepared for you, including every meal. Whenever you want a drink, wine will automatically come to you!

If you feel good, it will taste like the sky. If you feel sad, it will taste like sunshine. If you are anxious, it will taste like the ocean. If you feel sleepy, it will taste like chili.

"If you come with bad intentions, you will receive spoiled food."

Spell Library

Learning spells is the first step toward becoming a real wizard. Our library has a wide range of spell books, and everyone who visits Wizard Land can access them.

Clean your house automatically, shield yourself from harm, or instantly forget unhappy memories. These things can all be achieved with a single spell.

"Life is a constant journey of learning and exploration."

Time Confusion

Have you ever been certain that today was Sunday, only to discover it was Monday? In other places, you might have remembered incorrectly, but if that happens in Wizard Land, you are probably right.

Some living rooms can accelerate time. Do not worry about getting older, because your age will not accelerate with it. The people around you may not be so lucky. In other words, it is time travel, even if only for one day.

"If you happen to reach the end of the world, do not blame us."

Soul Swap

What if you became someone else while you slept? Would you play tricks on others? Would you rush to find your body?

In the magic living room, strange spells are always at work. You may even become someone you do not know at all. Do not worry; this usually does not last long, only 10 years at most :)

Our wizards can help you return to normal, but only if you can find your body.

"To protect the secret, you will not keep anyone else's memories."
